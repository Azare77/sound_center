# 🎵 Sound Center

Sound Center is a modern, open-source audio player designed to bring your music, podcasts, and online audio together in one seamless experience.

Whether you're managing your personal music library, listening to your favorite podcasts, exploring online radio streams, or discovering music on SoundCloud, Sound Center gives you a simple, smooth, and customizable way to enjoy your audio.

## ✨ Features

### 🎧 Local Music Player
- Play and manage music stored on your device.
- Manually edit audio metadata and tags.
- Automatically identify tracks and fix metadata using AcoustID and iTunes.

### 🎙️ Podcast Player
- Play and manage your favorite podcasts alongside your music library.

### 🌐 Stream Player
- Discover, search, play, and manage online audio streams.

### ☁️ Cloud Player
- Search, play, and manage DRM-free audio from SoundCloud directly within Sound Center.

## 🚀 What's Coming Next?
