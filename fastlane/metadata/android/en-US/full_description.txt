<p>
Sound Center is a modern open-source audio player that combines local music playback with advanced streaming capabilities in a single application.
</p>
<p>
Whether you want to manage your offline audio library or listen to your favorite podcasts and streams, Sound Center provides all the features you need through a simple, smooth, and user-friendly experience.
</p>
<p><b>Current Features:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>
<b>Local Music Player:</b>
High-quality playback of audio files stored on your device.
</li>
<li>
<b>Podcast Player:</b>
Easily play and manage your favorite podcasts alongside your personal music collection.
</li>
<li>
<b>Stream Player:</b>
Search, play, and manage online audio streams.
</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Planned Features for Future Versions:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>
<b>Online Music Service Integration:</b>
Support for services such as YouTube Music and SoundCloud in future releases.
</li>
<li>
<b>Additional Tools:</b>
Features such as lyrics search, tag editing, downloading, and more useful utilities.
</li>
</ul>
<p>
Join us on GitHub, contribute to the development, report issues, and follow the progress of Sound Center.
</p>
