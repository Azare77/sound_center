🎵 Sound Center is a modern, open-source audio player built to bring your music, podcasts, and online audio together in one seamless experience.

Whether you're managing your personal music library, catching up on your favorite podcasts, or discovering new online audio, Sound Center gives you a simple, smooth, and powerful way to enjoy your content—all in one place.

✨ Current Features:

🎧 Local Music Player:
Enjoy high-quality playback of the audio files stored on your device and keep your personal music library at your fingertips.

🎙️ Podcast Player:
Easily play and manage your favorite podcasts alongside your personal music collection.

🌐 Stream Player:
Discover, search, play, and manage online audio streams from a single, convenient interface.

☁️ Cloud Player:
Search, play, and manage DRM-free audio from SoundCloud directly within Sound Center.

🚀 Planned Features for Future Versions:

🛠️ Quality-of-Life Improvements:
We're working on even more useful tools and features, including lyrics search, tag editing, downloading, song detection, and more.

💡 Sound Center is continuously evolving. Join us on GitHub, contribute to the project, report issues, and follow the journey as Sound Center grows.
